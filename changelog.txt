Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-18
  Features:
    - Logistic chests over chains: every post serves the logistic chests next to it (any logistic-container, modded
      ones too). Requester and buffer chests get what they ask for from the chain network in vanilla's order
      (active provider, storage, buffer, passive provider); active providers and requesters' trash empty into
      storage. Nothing is over-delivered: packages on their way count towards the request.
    - Chests in a logistic network with robots are left to the robots (map setting to let chains help).
    - A post sends about a yellow belt's worth (8-item packages every 32 ticks); map setting "Package size".
    - Alt mode shows which post serves which chest.
